Selected Projects

TheoClaw — grounded domain QA agent with an evaluation harness2026 – present
theoclaw.app · code

A tool-using agent that answers questions from the Bible and the Methodist tradition in Chinese, English and German, used by church members and pastors in Hong Kong and Tübingen. Hybrid retrieval over a compiled wiki plus BM25; scripture is filled in by a lookup tool rather than quoted from model memory. The knowledge base raised grounded statements from 67% to 92%; the answering model was chosen with a bootstrap non-inferiority test (margins fixed in advance) over 270 blind-scored answers.
